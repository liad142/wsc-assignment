From a conversation at the booth to a shortlist

The full pipeline in three diagrams. In this exercise the CSV files stand for the end of stages 1 and 2; the code implements everything from "Normalize to one lead schema" onward.

Stage 1. Capture a conference lead

The QR path is preferred because it brings a verified identity and consent. Whoever does not scan is still captured by badge scan or a recruiter's note.

Stage 2. Enrich and assess domain relevance

A badge shows a job title. Only the profile shows industry, skills and history, which is what relevance is judged on.

Stages 3 and 4. Match a role and surface connections

Every point in the score traces back to a job requirement and the term found on the profile.
